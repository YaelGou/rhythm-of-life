Privacy Policy — Rhythm of Life

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Rhythm of Life ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Rhythm of Life does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you log — cycles, symptoms, moods, habits, notes, and any other trackables — is stored only on your device. We (the developer) never see it, and there is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What data the app stores

All data you enter is saved locally on your device using the app's on-device database. This includes any cycle, period, symptom, mood, sleep, or custom tracking information you choose to log, along with your app settings.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it or use the optional AI feature described below.

What data the app does NOT collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not require or offer an account, login, or email address.
  • We do not transmit your health or personal data to the developer or any third party.

Optional AI insights (opt-in, uses your own key)

The app includes an optional AI insights feature that is off by default and requires two deliberate opt-in steps to use.

If — and only if — you choose to enable it by entering your own AI provider API key (for example, OpenAI or Anthropic) and then tap to request an analysis:

  • The app sends the specific data you approve, at the moment you approve it, directly from your device to the AI provider you chose (e.g. api.openai.com or api.anthropic.com).
  • Before anything is sent, you choose whether to send a summary only or your full data, and you confirm via a dialog.
  • Your API key is stored securely on your device (in the device keychain) and is used only to authenticate those requests.
  • The data is handled by that AI provider under their privacy policy and terms, not ours. We never receive it.

If you never enable this feature, no data is ever transmitted anywhere.

Exporting your data

You can export your data to a JSON file at any time from within the app. Where that file goes and who you share it with is entirely up to you.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored locally, deleting the app from your device permanently deletes all data you have logged. There is no server copy to request deletion of.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. No data is collected by us at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
